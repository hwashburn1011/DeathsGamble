# Death's Gamble

A browser-based gambling-with-Death game. Spin the wheel, win gold, dodge the skull.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Click **Start Game** and you're in.

- 12-segment wheel of fortune
- Land on gold to score, on damage to lose HP, on the **skull** for instant death
- `Heal` restores HP, `AGAIN` grants a free spin
- Hit `Spin` (or press `Space`) — see how many rounds you can survive
