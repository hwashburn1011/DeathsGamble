# Death's Gamble

A browser-based roguelite. Make your bargain with Death — spin a **boon** wheel and a **curse** wheel — then descend into a Vampire-Survivors-style dungeon and see how long you last.

**Play it live:** https://hwashburn1011.github.io/DeathsGamble/

## How to play

1. Hit **Start Game**.
2. **Spin the left (boon) wheel** — gain a buff to your stats.
3. **Spin the right (curse) wheel** — accept a penalty.
4. Click **Enter Dungeon** (lights up once both wheels have spun).
5. Move with **WASD** or arrow keys. Auto-attacks fire at the nearest enemy on cooldown.
6. Pick up green gems for XP — level up to heal and gain damage.
7. Survive as long as you can.
